CHARACTER
A young woman in her late twenties with a slim build and pale skin. She has straight, shoulder-length black hair parted to one side, and dark brown eyes with a sharp gaze. Her outfit consists of a dark gray, practical jacket with multiple pockets, black fitted pants, and sturdy black boots. She wears a utility belt with pouches and a small holster on her right thigh. Her expression is reserved and she carries herself with a quiet, cautious demeanor.
