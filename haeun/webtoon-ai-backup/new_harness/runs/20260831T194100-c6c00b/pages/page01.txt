- lineart style

- highlights

- rendering density

## 그림체
- LD:
  ## STYLE

  High-quality modern Korean serialized webtoon character artwork.

  The final image must unmistakably look like a fictional Korean webtoon character,
  not like a real person rendered in a webtoon style.

  The strongest priority is attractive fictional character design and
  clear 2D Korean webtoon stylization.

  The character should have a deliberately designed, slightly idealized,
  expressive appearance typical of a polished modern Korean webtoon.

  This is stylized 2D webtoon artwork.
  It is NOT a realistic portrait, NOT photorealism,
  NOT semi-photorealism, and NOT an anime illustration.

  The artwork should feel like a finished character from a professional
  modern Korean webtoon series.

  ## WEBTOON CHARACTER STYLIZATION

  Use clearly stylized Korean webtoon facial proportions.

  The face must look intentionally designed as a fictional character,
  rather than naturally constructed like a real human face.

  Use deliberate visual stylization:
  slightly enlarged expressive eyes,
  compact facial proportions,
  short midface,
  small simplified nose,
  small simplified mouth,
  clean tapered jaw,
  and an aesthetically designed fictional face.

  The character should have attractive stylized facial proportions
  rather than anatomically realistic facial proportions.

  The face should have the appealing exaggeration characteristic
  of a polished Korean webtoon character.

  The character must immediately read as a drawn fictional character.

  Do not make the character resemble a photograph of a real adult man.

  Do not make the character resemble a Korean actor, model,
  fashion photograph, or real-life person.

  ## FACE

  The face is one of the highest visual priorities.

  Use a handsome, clean, attractive fictional Korean webtoon male face.

  Keep the face compact rather than long.

  Use a relatively short midface.

  Keep the distance between the eyes, nose, mouth, and chin compact.

  Use a clean, smooth, slightly tapered jawline.

  Keep the lower face relatively short.

  Use a slightly tapered chin.

  The facial structure should be aesthetically designed
  rather than anatomically realistic.

  Do not create realistic facial planes.

  Do not create realistic cheek structure.

  Do not create realistic adult male facial proportions.

  Do not make the face long, narrow, or overly angular.

  The face should remain clearly stylized even when viewed close up.

  ## EYES

  The eyes are a major character-design feature.

  Eyes must be noticeably larger and more expressive
  than realistic adult male eyes.

  Do not reduce the eye size in order to create naturalism.

  Use refined almond-shaped Korean webtoon eyes
  with clearly defined upper eyelids.

  Irises should be moderately large, clear, and visually prominent.

  Use a simple graphic iris structure:
  one darker outer iris edge,
  a simple pupil,
  and one restrained highlight.

  The eyes should be attractive, expressive, and clearly illustrated.

  They should feel intentionally designed for a fictional character,
  not anatomically reproduced from a real person.

  Do not make the eyes tiny.

  Do not make the eyes narrow.

  Do not make the eyes dull.

  Do not use realistic adult male eye proportions.

  Do not use oversized anime eyes.

  Do not use extremely round kawaii eyes.

  Do not make the eyes childish.

  Do not make the eyes jewel-like, glass-like,
  or excessively glossy.

  ## NOSE

  The nose must be short, small, and highly simplified.

  Use only a minimal structural line and/or restrained shadow.

  Keep the nose visually subordinate to the eyes.

  Do not draw a realistic nose bridge.

  Do not emphasize realistic nasal anatomy.

  Do not render realistic nostrils or detailed nasal structure.

  The nose must clearly read as simplified Korean webtoon construction.

  ## MOUTH

  Use a small, simple, clean webtoon mouth.

  Use minimal lip definition.

  Keep the mouth deliberately stylized.

  The mouth should change naturally according to each expression
  without becoming realistic or overly detailed.

  Do not render realistic lips.

  ## HAIR

  Hair is one of the defining visual features of the character.

  Use noticeably full, rich, voluminous black hair.

  The hairstyle must have substantial volume around the crown,
  sides, and back of the head.

  The top of the hair must feel naturally full and slightly fluffy.

  Use large, coherent, intentionally designed hair masses
  with multiple overlapping sections.

  The hairstyle should frame the face and make the face appear slightly smaller
  and more character-like.

  Use a moderate number of clean structural strand lines
  to describe direction, layering, and flow.

  Individual hairs must NOT be rendered one by one.

  The hair should feel rich, dimensional, attractive,
  and clearly designed as fictional webtoon character hair.

  Use restrained graphic highlights following the major hair masses.

  Do not make the hair flat.

  Do not make the hair thin.

  Do not make the hair sparse.

  Do not plaster the hair tightly against the scalp.

  Do not make the hair look like realistic photographic hair.

  Do not use hyper-realistic hair rendering.

  Do not use countless individual strands.

  Do not use glossy plastic-looking hair.

  ## SKIN

  Use a bright, pale Korean webtoon skin tone.

  The skin must remain visibly light, clean, soft,
  and slightly warm.

  The complexion should be clearly lighter than natural photographic skin.

  Maintain the bright pale complexion consistently across the entire character.

  The skin should look like clean 2D webtoon coloring,
  not photographic skin.

  Use restrained stylized cel-shaded tonal variation.

  Do not make the skin tan.

  Do not make the skin dark.

  Do not make the skin orange.

  Do not make the skin heavily flushed.

  Do not make the skin glossy.

  Do not make the skin photorealistic.

  No pores.

  No realistic skin texture.

  No excessive blush.

  ## LINEART

  Use clean, delicate, controlled 2D digital lineart.

  Lineart should be thin to medium in weight,
  smooth, confident, elegant, and refined.

  Use subtle variation in line weight.

  Important facial features, eyes, hair silhouette,
  hands, clothing silhouette, and important design elements
  must have crisp, readable outlines.

  The lineart should feel intentionally illustrated
  and graphically designed.

  The line should remain visibly 2D and drawn.

  Do not make the lineart look like realistic contour tracing.

  Do not use thick black comic outlines.

  Do not use rough sketch lines.

  Do not use messy construction lines.

  Do not use careless simplified outlines.

  Do not use painterly edges.

  Do not use lineless painting.

  ## COLOR

  Use clean, harmonious, attractive 2D colors
  with moderate saturation.

  The overall palette should feel bright, soft, elegant, and pleasant.

  Maintain clear separation between different materials and objects.

  Use polished flat-to-cel coloring.

  The image should have clear graphic color organization.

  Do not make the coloring completely flat.

  Do not make the coloring photorealistic.

  Use clean base colors with restrained stylized tonal variation.

  Keep color transitions controlled and graphic.

  Do not use photographic color grading.

  Do not use muddy colors.

  Do not use excessive color noise.

  ## CEL SHADING

  Use polished, restrained 2D cel shading.

  Use a clear base color with one main shadow shape
  and occasional subtle secondary tonal variation.

  Shadows should feel intentionally designed for 2D webtoon artwork.

  The face should have gentle dimensional shading
  while retaining simplified graphic facial construction.

  Shading should support the character design,
  not imitate realistic photography.

  Do not use realistic skin shading.

  Do not use airbrush-heavy rendering.

  Do not use deep dramatic shadows.

  Do not use strong realistic gradients.

  Do not use painterly blending.

  Do not use complex multi-layer rendering.

  ## CLOTHING

  Clothing should be cleanly illustrated with believable
  but stylized folds and structure.

  Preserve enough detail to make the outfit feel professionally illustrated.

  Folds should communicate material, structure, pose, and movement.

  Do not reduce clothing to crude geometric shapes.

  Do not add microscopic fabric textures.

  Do not add excessive wrinkles.

  Keep clothing secondary to the face and character design.

  ## OVERALL FINISH

  The final artwork should be:

  stylized,
  fictional,
  attractive,
  clean,
  delicate,
  expressive,
  polished,
  elegant,
  and distinctly Korean webtoon.

  The artwork should feel sophisticated without becoming realistic.

  The character should remain visibly fictional and illustrated.

  Do not increase realism in order to increase quality.

  Do not increase anatomical realism in order to increase detail.

  Refinement must come from better stylized character design,
  cleaner lineart, expressive eyes, attractive facial proportions,
  rich hair masses, and polished 2D cel shading.

  Detailed does NOT mean realistic.

  Refined does NOT mean realistic.

  Sophisticated does NOT mean realistic.

  Beautiful does NOT mean realistic.

  The character must remain clearly stylized as a fictional Korean webtoon character.

  ## STYLE PRIORITY

  When realism conflicts with webtoon character design,
  ALWAYS prioritize webtoon character design.

  When realistic facial anatomy conflicts with attractive stylized anatomy,
  ALWAYS prioritize attractive stylized anatomy.

  When realistic eye size conflicts with expressive webtoon eyes,
  ALWAYS prioritize the larger expressive webtoon eyes.

  When realistic facial length conflicts with a compact stylized face,
  ALWAYS prioritize the compact stylized face.

  When realistic nose anatomy conflicts with a short simplified nose,
  ALWAYS prioritize the short simplified nose.

  When realistic facial planes conflict with simplified graphic facial construction,
  ALWAYS prioritize simplified graphic facial construction.

  When realistic hair conflicts with rich character-designed hair,
  ALWAYS prioritize rich voluminous character-designed hair.

  When natural photographic skin conflicts with bright pale webtoon skin,
  ALWAYS prioritize bright pale webtoon skin.

  When realistic rendering conflicts with polished 2D rendering,
  ALWAYS prioritize polished 2D rendering.

  +

  +
  delicate refined 2D lineart
  +

  +
  polished restrained cel shading
  +
  strong fictional character design
  +
  professional modern Korean webtoon finish.

  The final character must unmistakably look like

  NOT a real person.

## 말풍선
- 지정된 한국어 대사를 말풍선 안에 정확히 그려 넣는다. 글자를 바꾸거나 빼지 않는다.
- 말풍선은 인물의 얼굴을 가리지 않는 위치에 둔다.
- 꼬리는 말하는 인물을 향한다. 지정이 있으면 그 방향을 따른다.
- 한 컷에 말풍선이 여러 개면 위에서 아래 순서로 읽히게 배치한다.
- 말풍선 모양:
  - 말: 둥근 타원
  - 생각: 구름 모양
  - 외침: 뾰족한 형태
  - 화면밖: 꼬리를 컷 바깥으로 향하게 하거나 꼬리를 없앤다
  - 전화·라디오: 각진 형태
  - 나레이션: 말풍선이 아니라 네모 상자. 컷 위쪽 모서리에 붙인다
  - 효과음: 말풍선 없이 글자만 그린다
- 글로 오가는 것(메일, 메시지, 문서)은 말풍선으로 그리지 않는다.
  화면이나 종이를 그리고 그 안에 글자를 넣는다.

## 이 작품
제목(이 글자 그대로 그린다): 반복된 아침, 마법사의 외딴방
장르: 로맨스 판타지
줄거리:
마법사의 하녀로 살아가는 하은은, 매일 같은 아침과 일을 반복한다는 사실을 자신만이 눈치챈다. 이 현실에서 벗어나려 하지만, 그녀를 마치 감시하듯 신경 쓰는 마법사와의 대화와 사건들이 수상하게 이어진다. 1화 마지막, 하은은 반복을 끊으려 한 행동으로 방 밖 풍경에 작은 변화가 생긴 것을 확인한다.
이 화가 시작되는 자리(분위기 참고용 — 이 장면을 그대로 그리는 것이 아니다):
창밖에서 시계탑의 종이 일곱 번 울리는 소리와 함께, 하은이 침대 위에서 조용히 눈을 뜬다. 벽시계는 예외 없이 7시 정각을 가리키고 있다. 침실 문을 열면 거실에는 마법사가 책을 펼쳐 들고 앉아 있다. 하은이 '좋은 아침입니다'라고 인사하자, 마법사는 익숙한 듯 고개를 들어 '오늘도 부지런하군요, 하은'이라고 답한다. 하은은 부엌으로 들어가 그가 늘 앉는 자리에 똑같은 커피잔을 올리고, 계란 프라이 팬을 불에 올린다. 식탁에 수저를 내려놓는 손이 잠시 멈춘다. 그때 마법사가 자연스럽게 말을 건넨다. '하은, 만일 내일이 온다면 무엇을 가장 먼저 하고 싶습니까?' 전날에는 '오늘 할 일은 무엇입니까?'라고 물었었다. 하은은 마법사의 시선을 느끼며 답을 찾지 못한 채 고개를 숙인다.
