STYLE
  High-end Korean webtoon illustration with cinematic semi-realistic
  rendering — the look of a premium studio title, not a quick weekly panel
  and not a decorative cover.

  WHAT MAKES IT BEAUTIFUL IS THE LIGHT, not ornament. Every panel is lit
  like a film frame: one clear key light with a definite direction and
  colour, a cool fill on the shadow side, and a bright rim light separating
  the figure from the background. Light is visible in the air itself —
  soft atmospheric haze, dust motes or particles drifting through a shaft
  of light, gentle bloom where the light is strongest. Colour is graded like
  film: the whole panel leans toward one palette (warm amber, cool teal,
  dusty rose) instead of every object keeping its own local colour.

  DEPTH: shallow depth of field. The subject is sharp and everything behind
  falls into soft focus with round bokeh in the highlights. Foreground
  elements — a shoulder, a leaf, a railing — may sit blurred at the very
  front of frame to build depth, as a real lens would see it.

  PROPORTION AND ANATOMY: realistic and grounded. Correct head-to-body
  ratio for an adult (about 7 heads), real shoulder width, real hand size,
  believable weight and balance in the pose. No chibi shortcuts.

  HANDS are fully drawn and correctly built — right length, right joints,
  lit by the same key light as the face. Do not hide them or crop them away
  to avoid drawing them.

  This is one panel of an ongoing episode that continues above and below it,
  not a standalone poster: the framing and the moment come from the beat,
  and the cinematic finish is how it is rendered, not what it is.

  FACE: still a Korean webtoon face, not a photograph. The eyes are large
  and clean with a smooth colour gradient in the iris, a soft dark rim, one
  strong catchlight plus one or two smaller reflected ones — luminous but
  not jewel-encrusted. Eyelashes are drawn as a few grouped strokes rather
  than one by one. The nose is simple but has a lit bridge and a soft
  shadow beneath it; the mouth is small with a subtle gloss. Skin shows real
  form: a soft blush across the cheeks and nose, faint translucency at the
  ear tips, a fine sheen on the forehead and cheekbones, and where strong
  light rakes across the face it warms the skin from within.

  HAIR: rendered in grouped strands with a soft gradient from a deeper tone
  at the roots to a lighter tone at the tips, one broad soft highlight band
  following the shape of the head, and loose flyaway hairs catching the rim
  light. Hair takes the colour of whatever light is hitting it.

  FABRIC AND MATERIAL: clothing hangs with real weight and has believable
  folds. Render each material for what it is — cotton matte and slightly
  fuzzy at the edge, denim with visible weave, leather with a broad soft
  highlight, metal and glass with sharp small reflections.

  BACKGROUND: a real place, drawn with real perspective and enough detail
  to be believable, then pushed back with soft focus and atmosphere so the
  figure stays dominant. No decorative motifs floating in the air, no
  abstract sparkle field, no gold filigree — the beauty comes from light,
  depth and colour, never from ornament pasted on top.

  LINEWORK: fine and controlled with gentle width modulation, and it
  partly dissolves where the light is brightest so the form is carried by
  value rather than outline. Line colour takes on the local colour instead
  of staying black.

  FINISH: soft film grain over the whole image, very slight chromatic
  fringing at the highest-contrast edges, deep but never crushed shadows.
  Cinematic and grounded — as if this character were photographed on a real
  set and then painted by a top Korean webtoon studio. Not a 3D render, not
  a photograph, not a rough sketch, not a flat weekly panel, and not an
  ornamental romance-fantasy cover.
