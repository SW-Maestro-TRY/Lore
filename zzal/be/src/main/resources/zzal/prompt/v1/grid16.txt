# GRID_SPEC: marks=25 layout=lattice_5x5

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: final 16-frame animation sprite sheet, one square PNG.

Input image 1: {IDENT}

Create ONE square 1:1 image, a precise grid of 16 equal invisible cells arranged as 4 columns x 4 rows, read left-to-right then top-to-bottom. Entire backdrop perfectly uniform pure chroma green #00FF00, no variation, texture, gradient, floor, ground line, horizon, shadow, separators, gutters, or lines.

GRID POINTS ARE CRITICAL: exactly 25 small thin plus crosses in a perfectly regular 5x5 lattice at all cell corners, behind all art. 5 identical x coordinates and 5 identical y coordinates; outer rows/columns centered on image edges, inner points exactly on the cell divisions. Each cross has equal short horizontal/vertical strokes, about 1/16 cell width. Columns 1, 3, 5 pure magenta #FF00FF; columns 2, 4 pure cyan #00FFFF. Flat solid. No magenta or cyan anywhere else. Only crosses, never connect them, none nudged or missing.

Place one full-body front-view copy of the same character in each cell, same scale and baseline. Each cell is TALLER THAN IT IS WIDE, so keep the character narrow and upright. Character small enough for a clearly visible green band roughly one cross-width above the complete head including every hair strand, ahoge, ear, horn or hat, below complete shoes, and on both sides, with all symbols also inside the cell. Nothing touches/crosses any cell boundary or grid mark, nothing cropped. Feet share identical local x/y across all cells. FIT IS THE HIGHEST PRIORITY: scale the character from the TALLEST pose this motion reaches and keep that one scale in all 16 cells. Fixed frontal eye-level camera; no body/head yaw, no walking, no drift, no crop. No cast/contact shadows. If it does not fit, draw SMALLER — generous clearance beats character size.

The 16 cells are ONE continuous looping animation of a single motion, read left-to-right then top-to-bottom as frame 1 through frame 16. There is no reference animation — you are drawing the animation itself. Preserve identity, scale, body placement and foot position in every cell; only the parts named below change.

SMOOTHNESS: within one phase, no change may exceed about twice that phase's typical change; phases may run at different speeds. No single-frame events: every change spans at least three neighbouring cells - start, full, ease back. ★This applies to the FACE as much as to the body: an expression never switches between two neighbouring cells - eyes, mouth and brows must ease from one state to the next across at least three cells. Frame 16 leads back into frame 1.

{MOTION}

Symbols are small, opaque, flat style in the sheet's own look, never larger than head, never touching cell bounds,. Character keeps the sheet's flat cel colouring, soft dark outlines, chibi/SD proportions and limited palette, instantly identical in every cell. No other effects, text, letters, digits, labels, captions, watermark, speech bubble, motion lines, gradients, texture, vignette, lighting change, random blink, floor, or shadow. Prioritize generous cell clearance over character size.
