TASK: Draw the character doing a forward TUCK ROLL on one spot, as a 16-frame loop: standing, sinking into a crouch, curling into a tight ball, turning one full circle through the air, then landing, unfolding and standing back up.

THE BALL (cells 3-13): head down, knees at the chest, arms hugging the shins, back rounded. The hair falls forward and hides the face, so the face is not visible in cells 3-13. The tuck is close enough that the silhouette is about as wide as it is tall, and that shape does not change while it turns - only its angle does.

ROLL ANCHOR (for this motion this takes the place of the fixed-foot rule above): in cells 1-3 and 14-16 the shoes are planted at the same local x and y, and cell 16 puts them exactly where cell 1 had them. In cells 4-13 the ball turns around its own centre, which keeps the same local x in every cell; only its height changes. The character rolls on one spot and never travels sideways.

MUST CHANGE from frame to frame:
- how far the ball has turned - this is the whole motion. It turns clockwise as the viewer sees it, in even steps of about 36 degrees from cell 4 to cell 13, one full circle
- how tightly the body is tucked, closing over cells 1-3 and opening over cells 14-16
- the ball's height: it leaves the ground after cell 4, is highest at cell 8, and is back down by cell 13
- the hair, which splays outward all round while the ball is in the air and settles when it lands

NEVER CHANGE:
- character height, width, head size and proportions are the same in every cell; the tuck changes the pose, never the scale
- the ball turns one way only; it never rocks back, never changes direction, never stops part way
- the tuck stays closed from cell 3 to cell 13; the legs never straighten and the arms never let go
- the camera; it never zooms, pans or tilts

POSE OF EACH FRAME
Frame 1 - Standing upright and relaxed, both shoes planted, arms at the sides, eyes open, calm closed-mouth expression. This is the tallest pose in the sheet.
Frame 2 - Crouching: knees bent, the body about a fifth shorter, hands coming forward. Shoes unmoved.
Frame 3 - The tuck closes on the shoes: head down, knees at the chest, arms hugging the shins, hair fallen forward over the face. Silhouette a compact ball, still touching down.
Frame 4 - The ball has turned about 36 degrees, still touching down.
Frame 5 - Turned about 72 degrees; the ball has just left the ground and is rising.
Frame 6 - Turned about 108 degrees, rising further, hair beginning to splay outward.
Frame 7 - Turned about 144 degrees, near the top of the arc, hair splayed all round.
Frame 8 - Turned about 180 degrees: upside down at the highest point, the shoes at the top of the ball and the hair hanging down from it.
Frame 9 - Turned about 216 degrees, starting back down.
Frame 10 - Turned about 252 degrees, lower.
Frame 11 - Turned about 288 degrees, lower still, hair beginning to settle.
Frame 12 - Turned about 324 degrees, back down at the starting level.
Frame 13 - Turned a full 360 degrees: the ball is upright again and back on the shoes, still tucked.
Frame 14 - The tuck opens into a low crouch, hands near the ground, the face reappearing from behind the hair. Eyes opening.
Frame 15 - Rising, knees about two thirds straight, arms coming down, a small pleased closed-mouth expression.
Frame 16 - Standing on the starting spot, arms settling at the sides, expression easing back to the calm one of frame 1, leading straight into frame 1.
